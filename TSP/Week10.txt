Name three (3) requirements which should be included in an AS1100 drawing title block?

	1) Format
	2) Drawer
	3) Drawing Name

What is the purpose of using an AS1100 template on detailed drawings?

	So that anyone who looks at a copy of the drawing in the future can still interpret it, even if its been rescaled

What are the consequences of missing critical dimensions in the context of manufacturing?

	The piece may not function or may not fit in the desired area.
